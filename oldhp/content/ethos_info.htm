Ethos


Ethos started as a small glide thingy shortly after I had changed from pascal 7.0 to windows and C++. It was a little rotating, textured cube which was not clipped to the znear plane.. though glide required that to show texcoords properly...but with the time it grew into a quite huge project with more than 10 ppl working on it (maybe the page is still here). It was an almost complete game engine showing joined indoor and outdoor landscapes at the quality of q2 and almost its speed. It supports client/server based networking, very ugly scripting, portal rendering, lightmaps, dynamic lights, 3d sound, heightmap landscapes,...

This was about 2 years after I had started it. But many of the ppl who helped me got a job (some in the industry) as they were quite good...so I lacked of ppl..and I had to pay internet per min..so we had up to 250$/month inet costs..so I was forbidden to surf..also I was the only coder (apart from eddy who did a radiosity renderer..or parts of it)and I had to manae everything..well..it was too much work and in the end it died like most projects of this kind...though we had been able to attrackt a lot of ppl with what we had..I never planned anything commercial or so..I did it for fun only..

The code was a big mess as it started as an experiment..the rotating cube you remember??..and was not planned to grow that big...and the ppl were all away...glide2.4 grew outdated and glide was on it's way to die already.. I followed the complete path of 3dfx..I liked glide very much as it was rather easy to use und not too much state driven like gl...this was the time where my poor old p133+voodoo1 was quite acceptable..
