Cart3D


Cart3d is a little project I started for MIDP1.0 enabld mobile phones for several reasons. My new phone "happaned" to to support it. Many job announcements asked for java and/or experience with mobile phones. Finally, there's no decent racing game for phones I know of.

It's still got some gfx glitches, no oponent and no menu interface :(
On the bright side:
-3D Tracks
-2^32 Tracks (geneated at runtime, the c/c++ test generator can be downloaded here with sources)
-no device specific code(should run on all midp1.0 or better devices)
-it's free :)

You'll prbably need an irda or other connection from your pc to your phone to upload the .jar/.jad contained in the rar file you can download here.
